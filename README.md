## About

I'm a Computing with Artificial Intelligence graduate who works across the full stack — designing the ML pipeline, building the backend that serves it, and shipping a frontend people actually want to use. Lately I've been pulled toward the research side of AI, particularly **LLM safety and jailbreak resistance** — understanding how models fail under adversarial prompting and what actually makes them more robust.

I like taking ambiguous, tangled problems and turning them into software that feels obvious once it's done.
