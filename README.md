## 👋 About

I'm a **Computing with Artificial Intelligence** graduate based in Nepal. I work across the whole stack: designing the data model and pipelines, building the ML on top, serving it through a solid backend, and shipping an interface people want to use.

I'm also a co-founder of **Fourth Wall Technologies**, a software and AI company building websites, mobile apps and AI solutions for clients in Nepal and abroad.

On the research side, I'm drawn to **LLM safety and jailbreak resistance**: how models fail under adversarial prompting, and what actually makes them more robust.

> I like taking ambiguous, tangled problems and turning them into software that feels obvious once it's done.
